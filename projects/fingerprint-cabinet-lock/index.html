Fingerprint cabinet lock

A course-team prototype that paired a custom PCB with a fingerprint sensor and a physical enclosure.

My role

Team member in an electrical circuits and devices course.

Tools & methods

  • Altium Designer
  • PCB design
  • Prototyping

The build

Our team designed a cabinet lock with a fingerprint sensor and an administrator mode for managing enrolled users. We designed the PCB in Altium Designer and integrated it with the sensor, display, and enclosure.

What I took from it

Designing for the enclosure made connector placement, wiring access, and assembly order part of the electronics problem.
