Mechanical design / 2018–19

My first telescope mount

A two-axis, stepper-driven telescope mount controlled from a laptop.

My role

Individual high-school science and engineering project.

Tools & methods

  • SolidWorks
  • Stepper motors
  • Gearing

The build

I designed and made a two-axis mount that could point a telescope from astronomical coordinates. Stepper motors drove the axes, with commands sent from a laptop.

The fair

The project placed fourth at the 2019 national science and engineering fair. The mechanics later became the starting point for my four-axis design.
